# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Independent Etsy sellers and aspiring shop owners who need evidence before committing time or money to a niche, store, or product. Their primary workflow is discovering buyer language, validating demand and competition, and turning credible signals into product and listing plans.

## Product Purpose

EtGen collects source-native keyword and marketplace evidence, preserves missing values as unknown, and helps sellers move from research to a focused store, product concepts, and listing drafts. Success means recommendations are traceable to dated evidence and improve as real shop outcomes accumulate.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and trustworthy. The product should feel direct and considered, with the clarity and restraint of Apple product communication rather than the noise of a conventional analytics dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense walls of copy, repeated metric cards, decorative gradients, inflated claims, invented fallback numbers, and generic SaaS dashboards where every element competes for attention.

## Design Principles

- Evidence before confidence: show what is known, when it was observed, and what remains TBD.
- Make the next decision obvious: reduce complexity until the seller can see the useful conclusion quickly.
- Trace every recommendation: preserve source, date, unit, and context behind analysis.
- Build trust through restraint: use plain language, honest uncertainty, and focused hierarchy.
- Turn research into action: connect validated keywords to store, product, and listing workflows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain keyboard access, visible focus, semantic structure, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, and text labels that do not rely on color alone.
