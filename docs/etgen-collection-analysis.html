One continuous evidence loop.

The system is designed as a queue, not a one-time report. Every pass can discover new language, deepen an existing market record, or replace stale observations with a newer dated snapshot.

01

Discover

Seed libraries, live search suggestions, daily search trends, and related queries introduce candidate markets.

02

Prioritize

New candidates, evidence gaps, stale records, and the oldest collected keywords enter a durable oldest-first queue.

03

Observe

EtGen requests marketplace listings, search language, and time-series signals without translating them into fictional demand.

04

Preserve

Raw rows, timestamps, geography, sample sizes, source names, and collection runs are stored in Supabase.

05

Analyze

Observed evidence is summarized. Profitability and opportunity remain TBD until a calibrated outcome model exists.

Fast enough to learn. Slow enough to trust.

Collection cadence comes from provider-reported limits or the source’s configured cache window. Work already spent processing a keyword is deducted instead of counted twice.

Analysis without false certainty.

EtGen draws a bright line between facts the providers returned, summaries calculated from those facts, and business claims that still require real outcomes.

Observed

Source-native facts with provenance.

  • Listing count when Etsy returns it
  • Sampled prices, favorites, shops, tags, and materials
  • Suggestion position and parent query
  • Dated relative-interest points
  • Provider quota and request timing

Summarized

Reproducible descriptions of observed rows.

  • Minimum, median, average, and quartile prices
  • Sample size and evidence completeness
  • Verified, partial, failed, or unverified status
  • Collection age and source coverage
  • Keyword relationships and expansion lineage

Still TBD

Correctly withheld until direct evidence exists.

  • Absolute search volume without an eligible source
  • Click-through and conversion rates
  • Net margin and expected profit
  • Opportunity and gap rankings
  • “Launch this store” recommendations

The recommendation threshold

Evidence collection is live. Profit claims come next.

A store recommendation becomes defensible only when marketplace observations are joined with first-party outcomes and exact unit economics.

Input 01

Shop outcomes

Impressions, clicks, favorites, orders, revenue, refunds, and advertising spend from the operator’s own Etsy shop.

Input 02

Exact economics

Production, shipping, marketplace fees, payment fees, advertising cost, and realized net profit for each product.

Input 03

Versioned calibration

A documented model tested against those outcomes, with a version attached to every score and recommendation it produces.
